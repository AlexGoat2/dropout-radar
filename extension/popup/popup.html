Privacy & data

Stays on this computer. Dropout Radar has no server and no analytics. It only talks to Canvas, Feedpulse and Portflow, which already have your data.

Read, not stored: your Canvas courses, assignments and hand-in times, grades and learning-outcome levels, attendance, and your name and email. These are loaded fresh every time the popup opens.

Stored in this browser: for Feedpulse, the checkpoint dates, whether you wrote a summary and the smiley. For Portflow, per learning outcome how much evidence there is and when it was last added. Your settings are stored too. No feedback text, evidence names or files are stored.

The percentage is an indication, not a decision. It is calculated from the parameters and weights listed above.
